One-image infographic summing up Saturday 21 November 2026 world news. Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image" bounty.

Six stories, each with at least two independent sources (full list in SOURCES.md):

1. Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain — Tehran says it hit two US positions; Bahrain says an industrial site burned, no one hurt.
2. Mexico captures "El Chapo Isidro" — the FBI most-wanted cartel boss is seized in Sinaloa; 11 dead in the violence that follows.
3. COP31 climate talks run past deadline in Antalya — a final deal is close, says the summit president.
4. Drone strike sets Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze in the Gulf of Finland.
5. Wolbachia mosquitoes cut dengue and chikungunya infections by about 69% in a Brazil trial of 5,700 children.
6. A 6.7 earthquake shakes central Peru — 1 dead, 16 injured.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to the output directory. The image is drawn with PIL (Liberation Sans) and renders byte-identically on every run.
